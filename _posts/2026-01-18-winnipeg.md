This weekend, I attended and presented at my first philosophy conference, which took place at the University of Manitoba. As it was the midst of winter, the campus grounds were covered in mountains of snow and surrounded by a -30°C chill that hurt to inhale. But inside the conference hall, the people were so welcoming, and the ideas shared so energizing, that I forgot all about the cold.
