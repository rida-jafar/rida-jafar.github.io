This weekend, I attend and present at my first philosophy conference, which takes place at the University of Manitoba. It is the midst of winter. The campus grounds are covered in mountains of snow and surrounded by a -30°C chill that hurts to inhale. But inside the conference hall, the people are so warm and welcoming, and the ideas shared so energizing, that I forget all about the cold.
