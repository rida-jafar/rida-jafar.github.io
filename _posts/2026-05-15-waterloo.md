As part of an ongoing ethics collaboration, my advisor and I visited [James Tung's lab](https://uwaterloo.ca/mechanical-mechatronics-engineering/profile/j6tung) at the University of Waterloo, where we tried out a non-invasive BCI headset and had an engaging discussion with James and his students about ethical challenges related to BCI access. We also got a tour of the robotics facilities.
