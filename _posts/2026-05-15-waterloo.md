My advisor and I visit [James Tung's lab](https://uwaterloo.ca/mechanical-mechatronics-engineering/profile/j6tung) at the University of Waterloo. We try out a non-invasive BCI headset, have an engaging discussion with James and his students about ethical challenges related to BCI access, and finish with a tour of the robotics facilities. I leave with a newfound appreciation for the work that goes into developing these technologies.
